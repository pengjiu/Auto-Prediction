joint/marginal composition research. HOLD automatic semantic certification,
independent review quality, full LLM automation and profitability.

ranking, while a useful composition is retrieved outside the title baseline's
budget. It does not demonstrate an independently measured discovery advantage.

thousands of retrieved pairs with thousands of hedge relationships.
